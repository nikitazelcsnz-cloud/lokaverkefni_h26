Valkyrie & Horses

The horses of the race

Horses were the heart of the Steel Ball Run. More than 1,500 competitors started the race, crossing sandstorms, mountains, rivers, cold, and heat. The demanding route tested every horse's strength and stamina.

Built for the journey

Valkyrie, Gyro Zeppeli's horse, was known for balance, strength, and stamina. He could travel long distances without losing speed, an important advantage on a journey filled with difficult terrain and little rest.

Fifty days in the saddle

The race lasted 50 days and covered 6,500 km. Horses faced constant strain, irregular nutrition, and dangerous conditions. Valkyrie was considered one of the strongest horses in the race and completed the demanding journey with Gyro.
