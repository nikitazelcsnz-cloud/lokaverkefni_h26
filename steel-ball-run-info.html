Steel Ball Run – Overview

Steel Ball Run is the seventh part of JoJo’s Bizarre Adventure and a reboot of the series, written and illustrated by Hirohiko Araki. It takes place in 1890 in an alternate universe where technology and fashion trends are slightly more futuristic than in the real world.

The first 24 chapters were published in Weekly Shōnen Jump under the title Steel Ball Run. Despite similarities in name, the work was not confirmed as part of JoJo until it moved to Ultra Jump in 2005. The series concluded in 2011, spanning 95 chapters across 24 volumes.

Steel Ball Run is a reboot of the JoJo universe and takes place in an alternate version of the first three parts: Phantom Blood, Battle Tendency, and Stardust Crusaders.

About the Race

Steel Ball Run is a 6,500 km race from San Diego to New York City. The winner receives $50 million. The entry fee was $1,200 USD, a massive sum in 1890.

Over 1,500 competitors signed up initially, but only about 20 made it to the end. The race took 50 days and traversed deserts, mountains, rivers, plains, and cities.

Plot – Brief Summary

Gyro Zeppeli travels to the United States to win the race and save a young boy from execution. Johnny Joestar enters to learn the Spin technique and regain the ability to walk. President Funny Valentine oversees the race to find the "Corpse Parts"— sacred remains that grant Stand powers.

Gyro and Johnny find the remains first and must fend off the President's agents who seek to reclaim them. Meanwhile, Diego Brando, a professional racer, competes for victory and, later, for revenge.

Key Characters

Gyro Zeppeli – Deuteragonist. Real name: Julius Caesar Zeppeli. Modeled after Will and Caesar Zeppeli from earlier JoJo parts. Arrogant, yet serious when facing danger.

Johnny Joestar – Protagonist. A counterpart to Jonathan Joestar. Paralyzed, but regains mobility through the Infinite Spin. Participates to reclaim his life.

Funny Valentine – President of the United States and the main antagonist. Orchestrates the race to find the Corpse Parts and gain power.

Diego Brando – Professional racer and a counterpart to Dio Brando. Makes a deal with Valentine to exact revenge.

Manga – Brief Overview

Araki wanted to write longer chapters and appeal to an older readership. He drew inspiration from American TV shows and movies and wanted to explore more complex moral issues. He traveled to the United States to sketch realistic landscapes.
